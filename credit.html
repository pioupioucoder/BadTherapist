02 / Information

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Bad Therapist is a narrative visual novel currently in development. The story and its characters will evolve as new chapters are added.

Your choices may influence the way certain events unfold. Some scenes, routes and endings may become available as you progress.

This is a work of fiction. The themes and situations presented in the story are part of its narrative.

03 / How to play

CONTROLS

  • Tap / Click: Advance dialogue.
  • Dialogue: Click again while text is appearing to finish the text animation.
  • Choices: Select an option when a choice appears. Your decision may affect the story.
  • Back: Return to a previous scene when the option is available.
  • History: Review previous dialogue when this feature is available.
  • Settings: Adjust the volume, import or export your save, or reset your progress.
  • Auto-save: Your progress is stored in your browser when the game saves.
04 / Important

YOUR SAVE DATA

Your progress is stored locally in your browser. Clearing your browser data or changing browsers may cause you to lose it.

To keep a backup, open Settings and use Export. You can later restore your progress using Import.

If you encounter a bug, try refreshing the page. If the issue persists, report it to the creator.
